🤖 Daily Tech Intelligence Report — August 11, 2026 (Tuesday)#

Verified key news from the last 24 hours (Aug 10–11). If no date is given, assume Aug 10.

AI & Machine Learning#

  • Meta’s Muse Glimmer: a 30B open-weight agentic model — Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Glimmer on Aug 10 under Apache 2.0. It’s a 30-billion-parameter dense multimodal model that can run an always-on AI agent on a consumer GPU (PC/Mac) — a cost-reckoning event for enterprises evaluating local AI. 🔗 VentureBeat | Computerworld

  • OpenAI: GPT-5.6 Luna rolls out to free users + GPT-5.2/5.3 shut down — GPT-5.6 Luna becomes the default model for Free and Go users this week (from Aug 10), with a “Think” button and unlimited text chat. Meanwhile gpt-5.2-chat-latest and gpt-5.3-chat-latest officially shut down Aug 10 — API developers must migrate. 🔗 OpenAI | Make (deprecation notice)

  • Google DeepMind open-sources WeatherNext Cyclones — per a Nature paper (Aug 6), WeatherNext 2 gives 24+ hours more lead time on cyclone track, intensity and wind structure; the model is now open source. (Betanews coverage Aug 10) 🔗 DeepMind | Betanews

  • Microsoft scaling up its own AI chip production — per The Information (Aug 10), Microsoft plans to “significantly” increase production of its AI chips (Maia family) — a big signal of reducing NVIDIA dependence. 🔗 Bloomberg

Software & Developer World#

  • OpenAI model deprecation effective (Aug 10) — gpt-5.2/gpt-5.3 chat-latest are closed. Apps still using them must migrate to the GPT-5.6 family (Sol/Terra/Luna). 🔗 Make Help | OpenAI Community
