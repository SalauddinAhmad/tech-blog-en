Tech Intelligence Report — 13 August 2026 (Thursday)#

The most significant technology news from the past 24 hours (August 12), condensed with analysis.

🤖 AI & Machine Learning#

  • Cognition AI (Devin) reportedly in talks to raise at a $40B valuation: Cognition, the maker of the AI coding agent “Devin,” is reportedly seeking to raise more than $1 billion, which would push its valuation to $40 billion — up from $26 billion just a few months ago. The round is reportedly anchored on ~$1 billion in annualized revenue run rate, underscoring the explosive growth of the AI coding agent sector. 🔗 TechCrunch

  • White House exempts open-weight AI models from mandatory security testing: The U.S. administration announced that open-weight models will be excluded from mandatory security evaluations — a significant turning point in AI regulation, as debate continues over accountability for open-source/open-weight models such as Llama and Qwen. 🔗 InformationWeek

  • Google’s Pixel 11 brings deep Gemini AI integration: The Made by Google event showcased deep Gemini integration across the new Pixel devices (details in the Hardware section below).

💻 Software & Developer World#

  • Visual Studio 2026 August update released: Microsoft has announced this update as the start of “a new era” — featuring deep platform-level AI integration alongside stronger fundamentals. AI-assisted development is now a core part of the IDE. 🔗 Microsoft Learn

  • No other major framework or language releases in the past 24 hours could be reliably confirmed in this category.

🏢 Big Tech Companies#

  • Google — Made by Google 2026 event (August 12): Hosted by Trevor Noah in NYC, the event launched the Pixel 11 series, Pixel Watch 5, and Pixel Tag — one of the biggest hardware events of the year. 🔗 The Verge

  • Tesla plans a $10B solar factory in Texas: Tesla intends to build a massive solar panel plant southwest of Houston, according to documents filed with the state of Texas — a major step in expanding Tesla’s energy business. 🔗 TechCrunch

  • Tesla + SpaceX — $16.8B “Terafab” chip factory: The two companies are jointly beginning construction of their own chip fabrication facility — a signal of vertical integration in AI hardware. 🔗 TechCrunch

  • Fermi (AI nuclear power) finally names a new CEO: After its CEO and CFO abruptly departed in April, the AI-driven nuclear power startup Fermi has appointed a new CEO. The company aims to play a key role in solving the electricity problem facing AI data centers. 🔗 TechCrunch

Upcoming:

🔒 Cybersecurity#

⚠️ Warning — Microsoft vs. security researchers: new Windows zero-day published: Despite Microsoft’s threat of legal action, security researcher “Nightmare Eclipse” has published a new Windows zero-day bug and exploit. This is part of an ongoing conflict — researchers previously disclosed six Windows zero-days, prompting Microsoft to threaten legal action and ban the researcher’s GitHub account. For developers and Windows users: this type of unpatched vulnerability can be actively exploited, so remain cautious until Microsoft’s next security patch arrives. 🔗 TechCrunch · The Record

  • Additionally, the security-testing exemption for open-weight AI models (above) adds a new dimension to the discussion around AI supply-chain security.
